# BIA Brand System

**BIA — Black Internet Alliance.** Maintained by ACND INT. LLC.

---

## The palette

**Gold on black is the system.**

The three BIA mother marks are **gold only**. There is no monochrome crest,
badge or seal — the alliance identity is always rendered in gold.

The five application marks exist in **both** gold and monochrome. Gold is
primary; monochrome is the secondary, for contexts that cannot carry gold.

| Variant | Use |
|---|---|
| **Gold on black** | All BIA mother marks, and the primary for every application. Hero placements, marketing, app headers. |
| **Monochrome** | Applications only. Single-colour print, favicons, watermarks, small sizes, and any surface where gold reproduces badly. |

For the applications, both sets are complete and finished. Monochrome came
first and stands on its own; gold was chosen afterwards for a more premium
read, and became the primary. Neither is derived from the other, so when an
application mark changes, update both — never redraw one to match the other.

The mother marks have no such pair. Where a BIA mark must appear on a
constrained surface, use the badge rather than reaching for a monochrome
version that does not exist.

## Mother marks

Three lockups, increasing in formality. All share the same shield and fist.

**Crest** — gold shield, black raised fist on a white field.
The core mark. Use where a single symbol is needed.

**Badge** — the crest inside a gold ring, "BIA" in gold serif above.
The compact lockup. Avatars, favicons, app icons.

**Seal** — the full institutional mark. "The Bureau of BIA (Black Internet
Alliance)" around the ring, the United States and Africa rendered in gold
and joined by an arc, "UNITED WE STAND" on a gold banner below.
Ceremonial use — documents, about pages, physical goods.

---

## The recurring motifs

Three elements carry across the whole system. Any new app mark should use
at least one.

**The raised fist** — in the crest, the badge, the seal, Blackflix and
BlackGPT.

**The US ↔ Africa connection** — the diaspora link. Gold arc in the seal;
continent silhouettes standing in for letters in Blackgram, pinned notes in
Blackboard, speech bubbles in Blackbook.

**Gold, black and white only.** White is reserved for the shield field
behind the fist. Nothing else in the system introduces a fourth value, and
the monochrome variants drop the gold rather than substituting a hue.

---

## The system lockup

The full-system composition places the seal at the centre with the five
application marks orbiting it on gold arcs. It states the architecture in
one image: the alliance at the core, the products around it.

Use it for the about page, investor and partner material, and anywhere the
whole platform is being introduced at once. It is not a substitute for the
seal — the seal identifies BIA, the lockup explains it.

Note that the seal keeps its white shield and black fist at the centre of
this composition while everything around it is gold. That contrast is what
holds the centre. Do not gold-fill the shield to match the ring.

## Application marks

| App | Construction |
|---|---|
| **BlackGPT** | Africa silhouette with a raised fist, set between "Black" and "GPT" |
| **Blackbook** | US and Africa in speech bubbles, elegant high-contrast serif |
| **Blackgram** | Camera lens within the B; US and Africa replacing the A and O |
| **Blackflix** | Film strip and raised fist within the B, geometric sans |
| **Blackboard** | Chalkboard field, US and Africa pinned as notes, network lines between |

Each encodes what its app does — camera, film strip, speech bubble,
chalkboard — combined with a diaspora motif. Follow that pattern for any
new app.

## Usage

**Do**
- Lead with the gold-on-black variant wherever it reproduces well
- Fall back to monochrome for single-colour print, favicons and small sizes
- Preserve clear space around the shield equal to its own width
- Use the badge rather than the seal below about 96px

**Don't**
- Introduce a fourth value beyond gold, black and white
- Fill the shield with anything but white
- Recolour the fist
- Stretch, rotate or add effects to the shield
- Place the seal on a busy background
- Use the seal below about 200px — the ring text stops being legible
- Redraw a variant by hand; gold and monochrome are the same artwork
- Create a monochrome BIA crest, badge or seal — the mother marks are gold only
